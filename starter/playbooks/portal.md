# Customer portal

Switches: database, sign-in with a customer role.

**Builds first:** the customer's own view: their rows only (orders, tickets, documents), filtered
on the server by who is signed in. Then the team's view of everyone.

**Asks (one card at most):** what a customer may change, if the request doesn't say. Default: view
only.

**Never asks:** layout, wording of empty states, the sign-in page (the door handles it).

**Done when:** a test customer sees only their own rows, checked as two different customers.
