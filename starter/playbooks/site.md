# Landing page or small site

Switches: content. No database unless a form needs one (then follow `form.md` for that part).

**Builds first:** the page, from the workspace brand or the existing site the person linked: hero,
what it is, who it is for, proof, one call to action, footer. SEO defaults: a title and description
per page, Open Graph image, `sitemap.ts`, `robots.ts`.

**Asks (one card at most):** the one thing to say if neither the brand nor a linked page says it.

**Never asks:** colours, fonts or layout (take them from the brand), which sections to include,
stock photos (use none, or the brand's own images).

**Done when:** it builds, every link works, and you checked it at phone width (390 px) and desktop
in the shared browser.
